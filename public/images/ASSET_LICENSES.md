## Pre-launch legal note

The Baku waterfront photograph's Commons page carries an Azerbaijan freedom-of-panorama warning concerning modern architecture. The photographer's CC BY-SA licence is recorded above, but the site owner should obtain legal review or a rights-cleared client photograph before commercial publication if required in the target jurisdictions. The airport file also contains a discrepancy between the Commons author field and embedded EXIF copyright field; this should be cleared with the source or replaced with a client-owned GYD photograph before launch.
